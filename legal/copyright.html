Review and handling of copyright reports

Zanora takes copyright complaints seriously and reviews reports concerning material accessible through its website.

Upon receiving a copyright report, the operator will assess the information provided and determine what action is appropriate under applicable law.

Where Zanora has the ability and legal responsibility to restrict access to reported material, appropriate action will be taken within the timeframes required by applicable law.

Where reported material is controlled by an independent third-party provider, Zanora may contact that provider or direct the copyright owner to the provider's reporting procedure. This does not remove any legal obligations that may apply to Zanora.

Where required by applicable law, relevant parties will be notified of decisions and actions taken.

All copyright reports should be submitted to the copyright contact address listed on this page.
