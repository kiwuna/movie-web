Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 12, 2026

Overview

This page describes the data practices found in Zanora's current frontend code. Zanora does not currently provide user accounts, registration, or a way to submit personal profile information through the site itself. It does not include third-party analytics or advertising scripts in the frontend code reviewed for this page.

Information may still be processed by the website's hosting provider and by third-party services that Zanora connects to. Those services may have their own privacy practices.

Information stored in your browser

Zanora uses your browser's local storage and session storage to remember preferences and locally saved activity. This information is stored on your device rather than in a Zanora user account.

  • Preferences: accent color, accent-color options, compact mode, navigation icon and visibility settings, category visibility, history visibility on the homepage, default player, and the ad-blocker notice preference.
  • Watch history: titles you have viewed may be saved in zanora_watched_history.
  • Watchlist: titles you save may be stored in zanora_watchlist.
  • Playback progress: movie and episode progress may be stored under keys beginning with progress_, TV state under tv_state_, and related temporary session storage.
  • Player data: the Viduki embed may send playback data that Zanora stores locally as vidukinet-Progress.

You can clear this information through Zanora's Settings page or through your browser's site-data controls. Clearing browser storage may remove saved preferences, history, watchlist entries, and playback progress.

Searches and movie information

When you search or browse, Zanora sends the requested search text, filters, title IDs, and similar parameters to its same-origin /api/tmdb endpoint. That endpoint forwards the request to The Movie Database (TMDB). TMDB receives the request needed to return catalog information. Zanora does not intentionally send your saved history or watchlist to TMDB.

Images are loaded directly from TMDB image servers and, when no image is available, occasionally from placehold.co. Requests to those services may include standard technical information such as IP address and browser details.

Cookies and similar technologies

The Zanora frontend code reviewed for this policy does not set cookies through document.cookie or a cookie-management library, and it does not include a first-party analytics cookie. Browser local storage and session storage are not cookies.

Third-party services contacted by Zanora may set cookies, use local storage, collect device or browser information, or process usage data under their own policies. This can happen when fonts are loaded, trailers are embedded, metadata images are requested, or a third-party video player is opened.

Third-party services

Zanora may connect to the following categories of third-party services:

  • The Movie Database (TMDB): movie and TV metadata, search results, credits, artwork, videos, and related catalog information.
  • Google Fonts: font files and styles used to display the website.
  • YouTube: trailers and clips embedded through youtube-nocookie.com.
  • Third-party video players: playback sources such as VidSrc, VidRock, VidUp, Viduki, VidStuck, VidFast, VidNest, and VidApi, depending on the selected player and availability.
  • Image placeholders: placehold.co is used as a fallback when TMDB artwork is unavailable.

When you open a trailer, clip, or playback source, your browser connects to that provider. The provider may process your IP address, browser or device information, playback interactions, cookies, and other data. Zanora does not control those services or their privacy practices, and you should review the provider's own terms and privacy policy before using them.

Server and hosting data

Like most websites, requests to Zanora may be processed by the hosting provider and its infrastructure. That provider may process standard technical information such as IP address, browser type, requested URL, and request time for security, delivery, and troubleshooting purposes. The applicable retention period depends on the hosting provider and the operator's configuration.

Data retention and deletion

Browser-stored preferences, history, watchlist entries, and progress remain until you remove them, clear site data, or delete them in Zanora Settings. Zanora does not currently operate a user account system from which stored browser data can be deleted remotely.

Requests involving server or hosting logs, TMDB, Google, YouTube, or a third-party video provider should be directed to the operator of that service. For Zanora-specific privacy questions, contact core.support.help@gmail.com.

Children

Zanora is a general-audience catalog and does not knowingly collect personal information from children through its own forms. Third-party services reached through Zanora may have different age requirements and privacy practices.

Changes to this policy

Zanora may update this page when the website or its services change. The date above shows when this version was last updated.
