About Zanora

Last updated: November 12, 2026

Zanora is a movie and television discovery website designed to help visitors explore films and TV shows. Visitors can browse and search titles, view information such as cast, genres, ratings, and release dates, maintain a local watchlist and viewing history, and discover trailers and clips.

What Zanora provides

  • Movie and TV show browsing and search.
  • Title details, artwork, credits, genres, ratings, and related recommendations.
  • Local watch history, watchlist, and playback-progress features.
  • Trailer and clip discovery through embedded third-party services.

Content and services
